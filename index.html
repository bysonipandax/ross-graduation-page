Why This Matters

A mispronounced name is the one thing every graduate remembers.

Graduation is one of the most photographed, filmed, and replayed days of a student's life. For names outside the "default" pronunciation set, a fumbled reading on stage can turn a proud moment into an awkward one — in front of family, friends, and a room full of cameras.

Universities want every graduate to feel seen. A trained, careful name reader is how that happens.
